# Mudlet: Where Developers Dwell

**Mudlet** is a hardcore, developer-themed MUD built for those who demand challenge, strategy, and a truly reactive world. Step into a digital reality where code comes alive—and often tries to kill you.

## 🚀 Key Features

### ⚔️ Tactical Attribute-Based Combat
*   **Deep Stats:** Combat is defined by four core attributes: **Intellect, Speed, Creativity, and Endurance**.
*   **Dynamic Bonuses:** Your primary class attribute provides direct damage bonuses. **Creativity** boosts your raw attack power, while high **Speed (15+)** grants a passive 20% dodge chance.
*   **Damage Mitigation:** Armor provides **Armor Class (AC)**, acting as direct damage reduction.
*   **Independent Combat Ticks:** Every mob and player acts independently. Face multiple mobs, and you'll be swarmed by their independent attack cycles.
*   **Boss Mechanics:** Bosses feature high HP, scripted special attacks, and world-wide victory broadcasts.

### 💀 Survival & Fatigue System
*   **Stamina:** Depletes with every move and combat action. Low stamina (-20% Dmg, +20% Dmg Taken) or high stamina (+10% Dmg) dynamically shifts combat effectiveness.
*   **Hunger & Thirst:** Requires active management. Consumables like food and drink satisfy hunger/thirst and provide essential regeneration buffs for HP, MP, and Stamina.
*   **Hardcore Death:** Dying leaves a corpse with all inventory and gear. You must recover your items via a "corpse run" before they decay (60s).

### 🎭 Interactive World
*   **NPC Dialogue:** Every NPC has unique flavor text and provides interactive help for game systems.
*   **Cyan NPCs:** Friendly characters are clearly marked in Cyan and are protected from accidental attacks.
*   **Wandering Mobs:** The world is alive; mobs roam between rooms periodically.

### 🛠️ Administrative Control
*   **Total Oversight:** Admins possess a powerful suite of tools: `teleport`, `kick`, `ban`, `spawn`, and `shutdown`.
*   **Persistent World:** Dropped items and corpses are saved and restored across server restarts.

## 🎮 Master Your Arsenal

| Command | Shorthand | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `look` | `l` | Survey the area and spot mobs, players, and loot. |
| `north, south, east, west` | `n, s, e, w` | Traverse the grid-based world. |
| `score` | `sc` | Track your attributes, EXP, Survival Stats, and Combat Power. |
| `equipment` | `eq` | View your current gear, mod bonuses, and slot usage. |
| `inventory` | `i, inv` | Manage your carrying capacity. |
| `kill <target>` | `k` | Enter combat with a hostile mob. |
| `flee` | `run` | Panic-escape combat to an adjacent room. |
| `talk <npc>` | - | Interact with NPCs for info, trade, or training. |
| `eat/drink <item>` | - | Satisfy hunger/thirst and replenish stats. |
| `quit` | - | Safely save your progress and disconnect. |
| `get/drop <item>` | - | Move loot between your bag and the room. |
| `wear/remove <item>` | `equip/unequip` | Manage your armor and weapon sets. |
| `loot <corpse>` | - | Recover your lost items from your own remains. |
| `train <list/attr/ability>`| - | Spend TP to grow your character's stats or skills. |
| `say <msg>` | - | Communicate with those nearby. |
